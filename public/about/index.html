Independent in spirit. Digital by design. Connected by culture. KIZAZI RADIO exists to reflect the energy, interests and changing world of a new generation.

THIS IS US.
A platform for sound, stories and perspectives that deserve to be heard.

We believe radio should move with the people it serves — not stay stuck in the moment it was created.

KIZAZI RADIO brings together music, entertainment, information and meaningful conversations in one digital space. Our programming explores contemporary sounds, entertainment, social issues, current affairs, sports, relationships and lifestyle.

We connect audiences from different backgrounds through music, ideas and conversation, without being limited by geography. The platform is built around the belief that the next generation should see its interests, questions and creativity reflected in the media it consumes.

We are here to entertain, inform and educate — while making room for fresh voices, new perspectives and the culture taking shape around us.

Founder & Creative Director

TELESPHORY
DEOGRATIUS MFUMU.

Telesphory Deogratius Mfumu is a creative and digital media professional with a background in graphic design, digital marketing, social media management and media content development.

His professional experience spans creative design, broadcasting and digital media. He is interested in how media, technology and creativity can be used to tell stories, connect audiences and create meaningful experiences.

He founded KIZAZI RADIO on the belief that the new generation deserves a digital radio platform that reflects its identity, interests, culture and changing world. As founder, he leads the station's creative direction and development, bringing together media, branding and digital communication experience to build an independent online radio platform with a global outlook.
