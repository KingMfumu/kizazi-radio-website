TELESphory DEOGRATIUS MFUMU · FOUNDER

Telesphory Deogratius Mfumu is a creative and digital media professional with a background in graphic design, digital marketing, social media management and media content development.

His professional experience spans creative design, broadcasting and digital media. He is interested in how media, technology and creativity can be used to tell stories, connect audiences and create meaningful experiences.

He founded KIZAZI RADIO on the belief that the new generation deserves a digital radio platform that reflects its identity, interests, culture and changing world. As founder, he leads the station's creative direction and development, bringing together media, branding and digital communication experience to build an independent online radio platform with a global outlook.

“KIZAZI RADIO is more than a broadcasting project; it is a platform for music, ideas, conversations and voices that deserve to be heard.”
