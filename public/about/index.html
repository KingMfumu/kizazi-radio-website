02 / Who we are

A DIFFERENT KIND OF RADIO.

KIZAZI RADIO is a digital radio and youth culture platform built around the interests, ideas and everyday experiences of a new generation. We bring together music, entertainment, information, sports, lifestyle and podcasts in one connected experience. We believe radio should move with its audience, speak its language and create room for fresh voices. We are digital by nature, open to the world and always tuned in to what comes next.

Telesphory Deogratius Mfumu · Founder & Creative Director

KIZAZI RADIO was founded by Telesphory Deogratius Mfumu, a creative professional with a background in graphic design, digital marketing, social media and media content development. The idea is rooted in a simple belief: the next generation deserves media that understands its interests, reflects its energy and gives its voices room to be heard. KIZAZI RADIO is a platform built to turn that belief into an ongoing sound and media experience.
