01 / Who we are

BUILT FOR THE NOW. CONNECTED TO WHAT'S NEXT.

KIZAZI RADIO is an online radio platform created to connect with a generation that is constantly moving, creating, questioning and discovering.

We believe radio should be more than something playing in the background. It should be a space where music meets identity, where conversations reflect real life, and where fresh perspectives have room to be heard.

Through music, entertainment, lifestyle, culture and conversations, we bring together voices and sounds that speak to today's audience — wherever they are in the world.

03 / The founder

A PLATFORM

KIZAZI RADIO was founded by Telesphory Deogratius Mfumu with a simple belief: the next generation deserves media spaces that connect with its interests, its creativity and its changing world.

Built from a passion for media, design, music and digital culture, the platform is an independent initiative focused on creating a radio experience that feels relevant, engaging and connected to its audience.
