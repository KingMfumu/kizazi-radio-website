01 / THE INTRODUCTION WHO
WE
ARE.

WE DON'T JUST
PLAY THE SOUND.
WE MOVE WITH IT.

KIZAZI RADIO is an independent online radio station built for a generation that is constantly evolving, connecting, creating and shaping the future.

We bring music, entertainment, information, sports, lifestyle and podcasts into one digital space — connecting audiences through sounds, ideas and conversations that matter.

We believe radio should move with the times. It should reflect the culture, interests and everyday experiences of the people listening, wherever they are.

More than a station to tune into, KIZAZI RADIO is a platform for discovery, expression and connection. A place where the next generation can find its sound and make its voice heard.

CREATIVE & DIGITAL MEDIA PROFESSIONAL

Telesphory Deogratius Mfumu is a creative and digital media professional with a background in graphic design, digital marketing, social media management and media content development.

His experience across visual communication, broadcasting and digital media shaped the idea for a radio platform that brings contemporary sound together with the interests and perspectives of a new generation.

He founded KIZAZI RADIO on the belief that the new generation deserves a digital media platform that reflects its identity, interests, culture and changing world. He leads the station's creative direction and development, bringing together media, branding and digital communication experience to build an independent online radio platform with a global outlook.
