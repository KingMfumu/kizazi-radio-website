KIZAZI RADIO is an online radio and youth-focused media platform built for a generation that thinks differently, moves differently and experiences the world in its own way.

We bring together music, entertainment, information, sports, lifestyle and podcasts to create a connected media experience that reflects the interests and perspectives of today's generation.

We believe radio can be more than a stream of songs. It can be a source of discovery, a space for expression, a platform for ideas and a voice for a generation.

KIZAZI RADIO was founded by Telesphory Deogratius Mfumu, a creative and digital media professional with a background in graphic design, digital marketing, social media management and media content development.

His experience across creative communication and digital media helped shape the idea of a radio platform that connects contemporary sound with the interests and perspectives of a new generation.

His ambition is to build KIZAZI RADIO into a distinctive media brand with its own voice, identity and place in global youth culture.
