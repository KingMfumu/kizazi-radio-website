01 / WHO WE ARE

NOT JUST A RADIO.
A POINT OF VIEW.

KIZAZI RADIO is built around a simple idea: the next generation deserves media that moves at its pace, reflects its interests and makes room for its voice.

We bring music, entertainment, information, sports, lifestyle and podcasts into one connected listening experience. It is a place to discover something new, stay close to what matters and hear perspectives beyond your everyday playlist.

Our world is always changing. The way we listen should change with it. That is why we see radio not only as something you tune into, but as a culture you can be part of.

Founder & Creative Director

KIZAZI RADIO was founded by Telesphory Deogratius Mfumu, a creative and digital media professional with a background in graphic design, digital marketing, social media management and media content development.

His work across visual communication and digital media shaped the idea for a radio platform that brings contemporary sound together with the interests and perspectives of a new generation.

The ambition is to build a distinctive media brand with a clear identity, a curious outlook and a meaningful connection with audiences around the world.
