01 / The KIZAZI mindset

NOT HERE TO FOLLOW THE FREQUENCY. HERE TO CREATE IT.

KIZAZI RADIO is an online radio and youth-focused media platform built around the energy, ideas and sound of a new generation.

We believe radio should move with the times. It should entertain, inform, inspire and create conversations that matter. We bring together music, entertainment, information, sports, lifestyle and podcasts in one connected experience.

We are not just broadcasting content. We are creating a space for expression, discovery and connection.

Founder & Creative Director

KIZAZI RADIO was founded by Telesphory Deogratius Mfumu, a creative and digital media professional with a background in graphic design, digital marketing, social media management and media content development.

His experience across creative communication and digital media helped shape the idea of a radio platform that connects contemporary sound with the interests and perspectives of a new generation.

His vision is to build KIZAZI RADIO into more than an online station — a distinctive media brand with its own voice, identity and place in global youth culture.
